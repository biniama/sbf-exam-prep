Privacy Policy – SBF Exam Prep

Effective Date: April 21, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SBF Exam Prep – EN & DE ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it to prepare for the German recreational boating licence (Sportbootführerschein) examination. We are committed to your privacy. The App is a fully offline study tool and does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

The App does not require registration, login, or any personal information to operate. You do not need to provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

The App does not use analytics, advertising networks, crash-reporting SDKs, or any other third-party tracking services. No data is automatically transmitted to external servers.

2.3 Locally Stored Data

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device using iOS standard app storage (UserDefaults / local app container):

  • Your exam progress and quiz scores
  • Questions you have answered incorrectly (for targeted review)
  • Questions you have bookmarked / saved
  • Your selected exam type and filter preferences

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server or third party. It is stored solely to provide a consistent study experience across sessions.

3. How We Use Information

The locally stored data is used solely to:

  • Track your study progress and display statistics
  • Remember questions you found difficult so you can review them
  • Restore your bookmarks and filter preferences between sessions

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user information with third parties. The App contains no advertising and does not use any analytics or tracking services of any kind.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or cloud services. All content — questions, answers, images, and explanations — is bundled directly within the App and does not require an internet connection.

6. Internet Access

The App does not require internet access to function. No network requests are made during normal use. The App works fully offline.

7. Data Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data, the risk of a data breach affecting your personal information is minimal. All locally stored study data is kept in the standard sandboxed iOS app storage, accessible only by the App itself.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). Since the App does not collect personal data from any user, no special provisions are required.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is generally no personal data held by us to access, modify, or delete. However:

  • You can reset all locally stored progress, bookmarks, and preferences by deleting and reinstalling the App.
  • You can clear individual study data (bookmarks, wrong answers) directly within the App's settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
