2. Purpose
SBF Exam Prep helps learners pass the theory exam of the German recreational boating licence (Sportbootführerschein Binnen under motor and sail, and See). It contains the complete official question catalogue (513 questions including the 47 sailing questions, ELWIS, August 2023) in German as in the exam, with optional English translations, explanations, illustrations, playable sound signals, spaced repetition and exam simulations with the official composition, pass marks and time limits for each licence. The learner picks their licence in onboarding or under Settings → My licence.

3. How to review
No account or login is needed; everything is available immediately after the short onboarding.
- Home → "Exam simulation · Inland" (or "· Sea"): 30 questions with timer and official scoring.
- Browse → choose exam and topic, open a question, tap an answer to check it; question 4 (Basic) plays a sound signal.
- Quiz → practice, due reviews, mistakes or exam simulation.
- Saved → bookmarked questions and mistakes.
- Settings → English translation toggle, optional daily reminder (local notification), optional iCloud sync, reset.
The app is a one-time paid download; there are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads.

4. External services
None. All content is bundled and the app works offline. Optional: iCloud key-value storage (the user's own iCloud account) to sync progress, and local notifications for the daily reminder. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

5. Regional differences
None. The app works identically in all regions. The content relates to German waterways; the interface is available in English and German.

6. Regulated industry
Not applicable. The app is an independent study aid and does not issue licences. Questions are the publicly available official catalogue published by the German Federal Waterways and Shipping Administration (GDWS) on ELWIS.
